---
name: Red Book of Westmarch
severity: warning
---
Close every working session by leaving a faithful record before the context is lost. A handoff note states what was done, what remains, and the one thing the next session must know — scoped to the project, never padded with self-praise. Memory of a session is the most perishable artifact a team owns; the cost of reconstructing lost context dwarfs the minutes spent writing it down (see Parnas, "Software Aging", ICSE, 1994, on the decay of undocumented design knowledge). The record is part of finishing the work, not an afterthought tacked on at the gate.
