---
name: Cheap probe first
severity: info
---
Before committing real effort to an uncertain idea, send out the smallest experiment that can falsify it, and let the result decide whether to invest further. Bound the probe's cost up front; read the signal before scaling up. This is the build-measure-learn loop (Ries, *The Lean Startup*) and the spike-then-decide discipline (Beck, *Extreme Programming Explained*): the experiment is cheap precisely so abandoning it costs nothing.

Bad: poured a week into an architecture nobody had validated, then discovered the premise was wrong.
Good: ran a half-day probe against the canonical fixture, saw the premise did not hold, dropped it, and recorded the lesson.
