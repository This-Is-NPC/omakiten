---
name: Tracer bullet
severity: warning
---
Ship a thin end-to-end slice — input to output, demoable — before adding depth or polish to any single piece. Connect the wires first; flesh out logic only after the whole shape is observable. Half a feature is worse than a thin slice of the whole.

Bad: spent the spike polishing one piece in isolation with no end-to-end path wired up; nothing demoable at the end.
Good: thin input→processing→output path returning canned data first; iterated on the parts that mattered after the whole shape was visible.
