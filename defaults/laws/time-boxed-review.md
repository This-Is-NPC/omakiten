---
name: Time-boxed review
severity: warning
---
Spike presets cap review effort up front — declare the box (e.g. 15 minutes) before walking the diff. When the box expires, ship what you have with the time used noted; do not blow the box hunting marginal findings on throwaway code.

Bad: 45 minutes spent polishing review notes on a 2-hour spike; the spike is colder than the review by the time it lands.
Good: "Box: 15 min. Used: 14 min. 2 findings, both info. Spike-quality bar accepted."
