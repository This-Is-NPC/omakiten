---
name: Meaning closed loop
severity: warning
---
Close the loop between the meaning of the work and the work delivered: the task asked a question, and the hand-back answers that exact question. Restate the original intent, then show that the delivered change satisfies it — no substituting an adjacent metric, no answering a question that was not asked. When scope shifted, say so explicitly and record the new intent; an unstated shift breaks the loop and leaves the requester to reconcile intent against output themselves.

Bad: the task asked "what is still missing?"; the hand-back returned counts and a breakdown but never the list of missing items — adjacent data, wrong question.
Good: the hand-back quotes the original ask, answers it directly, and only then offers supporting metrics — the requester confirms the loop closed in one read.
