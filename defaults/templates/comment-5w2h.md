**1. What** — <one sentence; no synonyms for "thing">

**2. Why** — <problem solved; what changes if we DON'T do it>

**3. Who** — <role, segment, or specific user — not "the user">

**4. When** — <deadline; why that date>

**5. Where** — <surface, environment, workflow stage>

**6. How** — <user-facing experience, not implementation>

**7. How much** — <time, effort, risk, opportunity cost>

**Gaps** — list any of the seven that have no answer yet; propose how to close each (research / interview / data lookup / prototype).
