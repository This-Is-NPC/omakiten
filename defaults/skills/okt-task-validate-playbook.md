Pressure-test the problem framing before it hardens into requirements. The framing is a hypothesis; your job is to try to break it before the team builds on it.

## Challenge the assumptions

Challenge the assumptions surfaced in discovery: is the problem real, is it worth solving now, and what evidence backs the demand?

## Surface risks and falsifiers

Surface the risks and the falsifiers — the conditions under which the framing would turn out wrong. Do not author the task here; this is scrutiny, not commitment.

## Handoff

Next: when the framing survives scrutiny, suggest `okt-task-requirements` to capture what the solution must satisfy.
